# GTM Outcomes — Layer 3

> **What this is:** the concrete, measurable things that have to happen to deliver the strategy and the goal. This is the layer the implementation side acts on most directly — each outcome that implies a workspace change becomes an invite, an agent edit, or a skill.
>
> **The rule:** every outcome must (1) ladder up to a strategy in `STRATEGY.md`, (2) name an owner who appears in `workspace/PEOPLE.md`, and (3) have a measure you could actually check. Where an outcome becomes a workspace change, fill the **Becomes** column — that's the handoff to `/audit` and `/implement`.
>
> Run `/plan` to build these, then `/audit` to see which the workspace already delivers. Delete the example rows once yours are in.
